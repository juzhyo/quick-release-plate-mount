Two parts, one motion.

The base mounts where your plate screw already sits. The adaptor takes its place on the back of your plate. Line the plate up, press it flat, twist a quarter turn — three internal cams rotate into their sockets and the plate can't be pulled straight off anymore. Reverse the twist to remove it.

Fitting it in three steps

1

Mount the base

One screw, using your bumper's existing plate hole. Sits under 4mm proud so the plate still reads flush.

2

Bond the adaptor

Peel-and-stick pad on the back of your plate. Leave it to cure for a day before the first wash.

3

Press and twist

Line the plate onto the base, press flat, twist a quarter turn until it stops. That's the lock.

Why we didn't build this around a magnet

Airflow lifts, magnets don't resist it

A magnet only holds straight down. Air moving under the plate at speed pulls it sideways and up — a direction magnets do nothing about.

Wet steel loses grip

Water film between a magnet and a wet bumper measurably cuts holding force. A mechanical twist doesn't care if it's raining.

No steel backing plate to add weight or rust

Magnetic mounts need a steel plate glued to your bumper. Ours is two small printed parts — lighter, and nothing to corrode.

Before you order

How long does fitting take?

About 10 minutes for the base, plus a day for the adaptor's bonding pad to cure before you drive on rough roads or hit a car wash.

Can I remove the plate without tools?

Yes — that's the point. Twist a quarter turn the other way and lift it off. No screwdriver needed once it's fitted.

Will this suit LTA plate requirements?

The mount doesn't alter the plate itself, only how it attaches — but rules on plate mounting vary by vehicle type and jurisdiction, so check current LTA guidelines for your vehicle before fitting.

Does it work with a plate surround or frame?

Slim frames are fine. Thick or raised frames can stop the plate sitting flush against the base — measure the frame's lip against our spec sheet if unsure, or ask us first.

What if I sell the car — does the adaptor come with me?

The adaptor stays bonded to the plate, and plates usually stay with the car in most jurisdictions, so plan on ordering a fresh kit for your next vehicle.

Is the printed material rated for outdoor heat and rain?

PETG-CF holds up to direct sun and rain without softening or degrading — it's the same polymer family used for outdoor-rated printed parts.
